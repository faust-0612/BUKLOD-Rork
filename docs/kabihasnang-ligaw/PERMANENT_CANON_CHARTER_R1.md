# KABIHASNANG LIGAW — PERMANENT CANON CHARTER (DRAFT R1)

Status: Owner-approved principles; operational canon register proposed. Planning document only; no app, manuscript, or production changes.

## Canon foundation
Kabihasnang Ligaw is a long-running, potentially lifelong narrative universe interconnected with the BUKLOD Character System. BUKLOD character identity, approved faces, items, badges, achievements and progression form reference foundations for narrative lore. A game-system change never automatically rewrites published fiction or changes a user's rewards.

## Source-of-truth precedence
1. Explicitly owner-approved canonical decisions, approved character visual references, and final released manuscripts.
2. Verified BUKLOD Character System source records for character identity, items, badges and achievements (interpretation requires creative approval).
3. Approved world, location, chronology, magic, and artifact registries.
4. Unapproved drafts, image concepts, and suggestions are noncanonical.

## Identity and visual continuity
Every major and special character needs a versioned character profile and approved reference sheet before final illustration. Preserve recognizable face, distinctive features, body proportions, and established world locations; fantasy costumes, magical effects and scene conditions can vary only consistently with approved lore. Do not infer exact approved faces from names or generic AI-generated portraits. Maintain scene-by-scene visual anchors and asset provenance.

## Registries
Maintain stable IDs and version history for characters, locations, factions, magic laws, items/artifacts, badges, events, chronology, relationships, and illustration references. Every entry records status (proposed/approved/retired), approval, source, first appearance, continuity constraints and dependencies. Cross-reference BUKLOD item/badge identifiers rather than inventing live records.

## Publication and immutability
A draft is not approved merely because a workflow is approved. Require explicit owner approval of final manuscript and artwork before scheduling. Preserve previously released chapters and their historical canon. Changes to canon are additive or explained in-story; formal corrections need an approved errata entry, never silent rewrites. Protect unreleased chapters from member access. System Admin preview is not publication. No OTA or production changes from this charter alone.

## Expansion
The five initial protagonists and eight planned special characters share one universe. New generations, eras, regions and story arcs must reconcile with the existing timeline and canon registry. No forced dependence on transient app UI, live points, or gameplay mechanics.
