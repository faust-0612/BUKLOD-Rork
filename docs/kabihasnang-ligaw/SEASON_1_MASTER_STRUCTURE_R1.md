# KABIHASNANG LIGAW — SEASON 1 MASTER STRUCTURE R1
Status: Owner-approved structural canon (5 leads, 12 interconnected series, one season arc). Story beats below are PROPOSALS, not yet manuscript canon. This is planning only, no production changes.

## Structural lock
Season 1 has five protagonists: Phan (19), Quin (20), Van (15), Christian (12), Lhen (25). Twelve interconnected narrative series, each with its own complete arc, culminating in one season-wide resolution. A series is NOT a chapter. Series 1 is Ang Lupang Hindi Namamatay, currently planned as Panimula + 30 chapters; later series lengths are not yet fixed.

## Provisional twelve-series arc (titles not yet approved)
01. Ang Lupang Hindi Namamatay — awaken the mystery and establish consequences.
02. Quin-centered exploration — uncover the world's hidden pattern.
03. Van-centered coming-of-age — test the cost of knowledge.
04. Christian-centered discovery — reveal an overlooked connection.
05. Lhen-centered legacy — expose the earlier generation's choices.
06. The first convergence — five paths intersect with incomplete trust.
07. The divided covenant — values and loyalties fracture the group.
08. The relic trail — item/badge histories acquire concrete stakes.
09. The reversal — an apparent victory reveals a deeper threat.
10. The lost chronicle — reconcile timeline, locations and memory.
11. The gathering — pay off clues and reunite the leads.
12. Season finale — resolve the season's central question while seeding the next.

## Required before each manuscript approval
For each series record: central question, beginning/middle/end, five-character status at entry and exit, location continuity, item/badge provenance, rules of magic, timeline dates, relationship changes, foreshadowing/payoff links, irreversible consequences, and what remains hidden. Every chapter must point to its parent series and story-beat IDs.
