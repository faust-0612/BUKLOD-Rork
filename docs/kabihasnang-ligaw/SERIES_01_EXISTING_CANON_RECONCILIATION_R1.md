## Identity / hierarchy
Season 1 = five protagonists (Phan 19, Quin 20, Van 15, Christian 12, Lhen 25) across twelve interconnected series.
Series 01 = **Ang Lupang Hindi Namamatay**. Its Panimula is **Ang Daigdig na May Sariling Alaala**; Chapter 1 is **Ang Gabi ng Pag-ahon**. The intended structure is Panimula + Chapters 1–30. Do not rename this existing series to the thematic working label 'Pagkamulat'. 'Pagkamulat' is its internal thematic function only.

## Existing anchors to preserve
Panimula and Chapter 1 are already member-readable in the app and are not to be rewritten.
Chapter 1 source storyboard:
- Phan in Lunawen storehouse; tied stalks, rolling grain jar, hairline floor crack.
- Mother and Tali at tilting homestead; massive gray root with pale green veins. Father remains a VOICE, not a visible ghost.
- Child at fissure, Phan reaching, tangled underground roots and light. Child initially looks ordinary.
- Phan's rope rescue; root at ankle; collapsing home; father's voice disappears.
- Closing water reflection / absence of child's shadow is a reveal ONLY at the end. Never spoil with earlier images.
- Chapter 1 is windy and moonlit; NO rain. Preserve spatial continuity and visual identity.

## Thematic 01: Pagkamulat, grounded in existing events
The living earth and the child's missing shadow are an observable anomaly, not proof that the world is a dream. The voice, roots, reflections and the cost of rescue seed questions of perception, memory, identity and responsibility. Phan's dilemma is whether to protect someone while acknowledging what he cannot know. Maintain both the literal stakes and the covert consciousness allegory; never announce the allegory to readers.
