# BAHL-CUR-001 — Integrated Curriculum and Learning Outcomes (draft)

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-11-24 | **Authority:** MZB Ministry Inc., proposed Academy Academic Quality Lead | **Publication:** Draft until source and academic approval.

This is a **non-formal** ministry curriculum. Completion records do not confer government-recognized degrees or academic equivalency.

## 1. Curriculum design principle

The **53 existing academy credentials** (32 certificates, 9 diplomas, 7 bachelor's-level program completions, 5 master's-level program completions) remain in `src/data/university/buklodUniversityCatalog.json`. Its code, threshold, direct prerequisites, tier and evidence gate are canonical; the separate JSON framework crosswalks each code to outcomes. A learning pathway is **not** a newly created lesson or state-equivalent academic degree. No UC is granted by downloading a certificate. Every qualification requires (A) eligible verified cumulative UC, (B) recursive prerequisite eligibility, and (C) all published source-specific competencies and required research/practice evidence.

## 3. Learning outcomes matrix

The framework records **16 theological/educational competency domains**: Scripture; doctrine of God; Christology; Holy Spirit; anthropology/sin; salvation; church/covenants; spiritual formation; ethics; Biblical genres; education/storytelling; discipleship/care; leadership/mission; comparative theology; research design/ethics; research communication/defense. Each must reference an actual published lesson, source version and rubric result; a proposed domain mapping is **not** evidence that a learner mastered it.

## 4. Minimal pilot syllabi (no new teaching content)

### C-S01 — Scripture Devotion (30 cumulative UC)

**Module source:** Daily Reading; **delivery:** existing reading, reflection and source-verification flow. **Outcomes:** after 20 unique assigned and approved readings the learner can describe central passage meaning and provide an original, relevant reflection. **Assessment:** review of each assignment and unique source record; original reflective response; verified identity and timestamp. **Completion:** at least 20 distinct approved assignments, at least 30 cumulative verified UC and publication/eligibility rules from canonical catalog. **Records:** assignment ID, automated verifier state/version, member ID, credit event, final gate decision. **No diploma** for individual daily completion.

### C-D01 — Christian Foundations — ABaKa (150 cumulative UC)

**Module source:** already approved ABaKa A–Z and applicable preliminary units. **Outcomes:** accurate explanation of foundational doctrines and evidence-based answers linked to Scripture and published content. **Assessment:** canonical unit-level validated completion and existing question/rubric rules. **Completion:** all published designated foundational units and preliminaries, historical ID reconciliation, 150 verified UC and any formal requirements in the catalog. **Records:** exact unit ID/version, question pass/result, authoritative BP/source linkage where required, policy version. **Blocker:** unverified legacy A–Z IDs are not automatically considered passes.

### C-F01 — BUKLOD Foundations (101–103; 250 cumulative UC)

**Module source:** official existing BUKLOD Formation 101, 102, 103 in their published order, plus applicable official covenants. **Outcomes:** responsible foundational discipleship; application of required 101–103 commitments, with appropriate evidence and validation. **Assessment:** official curriculum milestones, sequential stage verification and covenant/attestation evidence where specified. **Completion:** real 101–103 approved stage records and at least 250 cumulative verified UC. **Blocker:** missing old source records require review; no synthetic stage completion.

## 5. All advanced credentials

- **Diplomas (D01–D09):** thematic integration of existing certificates and published/source-verified research as specified by canonical catalog; they do not introduce standalone lecture requirements.
- **Bachelor's-level (B01–B07):** official 101–105 formation and relevant doctrine/Bible/education competence plus an original independently reviewed research design and specialization paper. No 105 completion means no bachelor's-level award.
- **Master's-level (M01–M05):** relevant prior bachelor's-level eligibility, official 101–107 formation with *assigned* 106 track, advanced doctrinal evidence, unique source-verifiable capstone and structured documented defense. No verified 106/107 or reviewed research means no master's-level award.
- **Teaching/ministry claims:** avoid representing story reading as professional teacher certification or 106 ministry formation as ordination.

## 6. Course approval and revision template

For every source module or new version record: **(1)** source lesson/published ID and owner; **(2)** intended age/audience/needs; **(3)** specific learning outcome; **(4)** Bible/learning references and license; **(5)** delivery method already in app; **(6)** assessment questions or rubric and integrity controls; **(7)** accommodations; **(8)** prerequisites/gates; **(9)** objective evaluation and test evidence; **(10)** human academic approval date and version; **(11)** deprecation/supersession mapping; **(12)** approval to expose it in catalog. Any field missing means *draft*, not approved instruction.
