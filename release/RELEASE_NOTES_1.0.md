# BUKLOD 1.0 — Initial Release Notes

BUKLOD 1.0 provides a unified ministry app for devotional growth, discipleship, prayer,
community interaction, church life, and authorized pastoral care.

## Member
- Daily devotionals
- Reflection with privacy controls
- Prayer requests and "I Prayed"
- BUKLOD community posts/comments/reactions
- Learning tracks and quizzes
- Church events/announcements
- Attendance/self check-in
- Ministries and serving assignments
- Church directory privacy controls
- Notifications
- Privacy/data-rights requests
- In-app account deletion
- Support and feedback

## Leaders / Pastors
- Group dashboard and caring follow-up
- Membership approval/assignment workflows
- Mentor/disciple review
- Church life administration
- Pastoral member-care profile
- Visitor/new-member journey
- Reports and analytics

## System Administration
- Devotional/content management
- User/role administration
- Privacy/moderation
- Pilot operations
- Maintenance/version controls
- Feature flags
- Pilot feedback
- Go-Live Command Center
- Incident/readiness/release-candidate/rollout decision records

BUKLOD participation indicators are operational and pastoral-care signals, not measures
of holiness, salvation, or spiritual worth.
