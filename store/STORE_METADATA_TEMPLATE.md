## Full Description — Draft
BUKLOD helps members grow together through daily devotionals, prayer, discipleship
learning, church events, community interaction, and caring follow-up.

Core experiences include:
- Daily Scripture/devotional content
- Personal and shared reflections with privacy choices
- Prayer requests with audience controls
- BUKLOD community interaction
- Discipleship and learning tracks
- Church events, announcements, attendance, ministries, and serving
- Member-controlled church-directory visibility
- Pastoral follow-up and member-care workflows for authorized leaders

BUKLOD participation information is intended to support care and discipleship. It is not
a holiness, salvation, or spiritual-worth score.
