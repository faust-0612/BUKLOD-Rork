# BUKLOD Arena — authoritative game design blueprint v1.0
Owner-approved direction, 2026-11-24. Baseline 264a5b0f6c96b49c65ba9c2612b088aa65500301. Unity DEV only; retain canonical Lux/Tenebra factions, Genesis Rift, 13 existing hero IDs/skills and approved art. Suggested terminology (Land of Covenant, Covenant Heart etc.) is provisional display naming, not a migration of identifiers. MLBB is a genre reference, not a source-code or asset dependency. No claim of proprietary parity.

## Status and execution rules
Existing source: fixed-step offline 5v5, 13 kits/39 active skills, basic combat and gear, minion waves, towers/core, village simulation, touch/HUD, stone catalog/preflight and undeployed SQL candidate. These are source observations, NOT runtime verification. All requirements below are acceptance targets unless independently verified. This batch adds offline tower retaliation/ramp and kill/death/damage-assist tracking. No production, OTA, build, real currency, database writes, online rewards or paid services. Preserve existing offline training and village variant; ranked rules are a separate mode. Do not rename hero IDs or override existing canonical approved kits. Numerical values are tunable proposals.

## 50 requirements and acceptance contracts
1. **Battlefield.** Genesis Rift: two factions, three lanes (Valor/Provision/Covenant), traversable wilderness and river, distinct concealment vs solid cover, fair mirrored paths, collision, fog, neutral objectives, protected Citadel/Core. Victory only by full enemy faction elimination, not core destruction; 15-minute reinforcement cutoff then elimination/overtime. Draw only for irrecoverable no-mobile-combat stalemate; test actual pacing.
2. **Roles.** Guardian, Vanguard, Shadow, Invoker, Ranger, Keeper; assign primary/secondary roles from canonical kits after balance testing, never replace existing abilities merely to fill role quotas. 13 heroes, no paid stat hierarchy.
3. **Combat.** Basic/passive/three active skills, attack range/speed, mana/energy, cooldown, physical/spiritual/true damage, crit, penetration, lifesteal/spell recovery, shields, anti-heal, regeneration, summons/transformation. CC: stun, slow, silence, root, knockup/back, pull, blind, disarm, taunt, fear, suppression, cleanse, immunity, untargetable. Every effect has eligibility, duration and counterplay.
4. **Micro mechanics.** Aim cones, skill shots, hitboxes, projectiles, windup/recovery, interruptible channels, controlled animation cancel, dash/blink/wall crossing, body block, area denial, kiting, target priority, input buffering, bounded invulnerability. Never tie simulation to FPS.
5. **Sacred Stones.** 13 character-specific acquisition missions, Kingdom and game acquisition, 100 BP each, one stone per hero match, uniform price; share/trade/purchase with transparent terms, combinations/conversions and optional hero-specific ban/counter configuration. Arena-only utility, no paid damage buffs. Counter loadout replaces another option; equal competitive alternatives available to all; never modify live inventory from offline.
6. **Draft.** Role preferences, matchmaking, ready check, at most one unique ban per team, alternating unique picks, fallback access for blocked picks, counter selection and loadout, reserve then settle at valid start. Ten picks fit thirteen heroes. Free training.
7. **Economy/equipment.** Separate BP, permanent XP, stones, match gold/EXP, seasonal rating. Ranked level 1 start; preserve existing training starts until mode split. Target 48 items across weapons, armor, footwear, relics, accessories, artifacts; basic/intermediate/advanced recipes, six slots subject to balance, unique passive groups, actives, buy/sell/preorder, optional beginner autobuy, counters. Permanent gear cosmetics only in ranked.
8. **Tactical abilities.** One free spell/loadout per match: Swift Passage, Divine Refuge, Cleansing Light, Final Strike, Renew, Shared Grace, Pursuit, Binding Seal, Wilderness Mastery, Guardian's Stand, Battle Fervor, Rally. Independent cooldown, eligibility and fair counterplay.
9. **Units/villagers.** Preserve canonical faction minion roles, lane wave timing, last hit, freezing, slow/fast/split push, siege reinforcements, anti-backdoor. Age-of-Empires-inspired villagers are functional, targetable workers with gathering, research, crafting, healing, training and tower upgrades. Dead workers cannot perform jobs. Core loss disables automatic worker resurrection, not surviving workers' paid jobs; retain distinct faction professions and balance them.
10. **Objectives.** Proposed 15–20 minute pacing: opening 0–2, first objective 2–5, rotations 5–8, major siege 8–12, endgame 12–18, escalation after 18. Ancient Sentinel/Colossal Guardian names provisional; leash/reset, buff ownership, respawn, objective secure/steal, single award.
11. **Towers/death.** Minion priority except eligible recent hero aggression, bounded ramp, early shield/backdoor protection, kill/assist/shutdown, reduced repeat bounty, respawn timers, recap, spectate, interruptible recall, sanctuary recovery. Death never consumes a second stone. Core destruction disables automatic reinforcement and respawn for that faction, but surviving units fight; victory requires all enemy heroes, villagers, minions and structures including active summons gone.
12. **Controls.** Joystick, attack/minion/tower buttons, hero portrait lock, manual aim, quick cast, drag/cancel, camera and minimap pan, sensitivity/layout, haptics, safe areas. No accidental casts.
13. **AI.** Farm, jungle, gank, retreat, combos, dodge, ambush, defend, coordinate, buy counters, contest objectives, manage resources, legal vision. Five difficulty levels by decision quality, not cheating.
14. **Modes.** Free offline training/duel/5v5 AI first; online classic/ranked after authority, custom, 3v3, spectator, cluster/org competitions; later cooperative boss, village defense, seasonal maps, championships, challenges.
15. **Rank.** Separate Arena rating from Circle Rank and Character XP; MMR, placements, seasons, leaderboards, mastery, promotion, fair outage/AFK protection. Proposed tiers Initiate/Sentinel/Vanguard/Champion/Ascendant/Covenant Elite/Legend of BUKLOD.
16. **Matchmaking.** Comparable skill, latency/region, party parity, role selection, novice protection, controlled queue widening, disclosed AI, reconnect and takeover, surrender, abuse/cheating/collusion detection. No forced streaks.
17. **Results.** KDA plus damage dealt/taken, heal/shield, objectives, towers, participation, rescues and CC; role-normalized MVP, recap, XP/stone/rating only after authoritative verified settlement. No QMP/cash linkage.
18. **Advanced UX.** Training dummy, mastery, builds, counters, replays/highlights, damage recap, leaderboards, pings, quick chat, friends/cluster invite, spectator, tournaments, favorites, hero stats, timeline, custom rematch, coaching.
19. **Identity.** Stones, character legacy, original premium artwork, living battlefield, community affiliation, covenant synergy, Kingdom acquisition. No imported ML names/art/audio.
20. **Fairness exclusions.** No random paid loot, paid combat power, fabricated players, forced losses, ads during combat, punitive daily pressure, excessive currencies, unjust sanctions or AI omniscience.
21. **Architecture.** Canonical Character System owns permanent identity/XP/inventory; Arena owns match state. Reusable simulation, abilities, effects, equipment, AI, navigation/vision, objectives, economy, animation/audio and networking. Unity presentation separate. Server authority online; no phone-issued rewards.
22. **Delivery.** Offline movement/map -> heroes/effects -> economy -> units/objectives -> AI/full matches -> stones -> visual polish; asset production parallel. Then online authority, reconnect, ranked/rewards, test/RC/Android. No premature release.
23. **Release gates.** Individually approved 13 rigs, distinct motion, real offline 5v5, responsive controls, accurate hits, AI, equipment/EXP, stone lifecycle, towers/jungle, original premium map/audio, online fairness when enabled, actual Android verification, existing app/ad/nav regression and exact build identity. Fail closed.
24. **Attributes.** HP/max HP/regen, physical/spiritual power and defense, attack speed/range, movement, crit chance/damage, flat/% penetration, CDR, resource regen/efficiency, lifesteal/spell heal, resilience/tenacity, healing/shield amplification, mitigation, vision. Validate ranges and finite values.
25. **Damage pipeline.** Base + scaling -> conditional mods -> eligible crit -> defense reduction/penetration -> mitigation -> shield -> HP -> eligible secondary effects. Proposed positive-defense multiplier 100/(100+effective defense); signed defense and caps require explicit tests. Avoid NaN, duplicate events and infinite reflection.
26. **Damage classifications.** Burst, sustained, DOT, AoE, splash, chain, pierce, return/homing projectiles, execute, current/missing/max HP scaling, reflect, delayed explosion, channel, enhanced basic/summons. Per-event crit/proc/lifesteal/tower eligibility.
27. **Skill primitives.** Charges, hold, recast/reset, mark/detonate/stacks, stance, transform, parry, intercept, trap/zone, tether, delayed return, companion, rescue, terrain, redirection, resource tradeoff; use only where consistent with existing hero identity.
28. **Interactions.** Define immunity vs untargetable, root vs dash, suppression vs cleanse, shields and DOT, strongest nonstackable CC, stack caps, dead caster projectiles, summon lifetime, revival cooldown policy, simultaneous lethals; prevent recursive proc loops. Current immediate-hit simulation is not proof of all these.
29. **Covenant Paths.** Valor, Wisdom, Courage, Swiftness, Grace, Precision, Stewardship; primary path plus three compatible talents, all free in ranked, mastery cosmetic.
30. **Counter items.** Anti-heal/shield, physical/spiritual penetration, percent HP, attack-speed reduction, barrier, DOT/CC/crit resistance, movement control; Covenant Blade, Sentinel Armor, Sealbreaker, Withering Thorn, Mantle of Refuge, Wings of Passage, Crown of Wisdom, Chains of Restraint, Aegis of Dawn, Mirror of Justice, Guardian's Oath, Phoenix Relic. Unique effects cannot stack or grant repeated instant revive.
31. **Vision.** Visible/terrain-hidden/concealed/camouflaged/revealed/last-known/untargetable states; team vision, detection and reveal on eligible actions. Server filters hidden entity data; no client-only hiding as anti-cheat.
32. **Wilderness.** Original Azure Stag (resource), Crimson Wolf (offense), Emerald Serpent (regen), Golden Scarab (gold), Stone Golem (resistance), River Spirit (vision), Wind Falcon (mobility), Sentinel and Colossal Guardian. Buff duration, ownership and death transfer explicit. No duplicate claim.
33. **Role economy.** Valor EXP, Provision gold, middle rotation, wilderness specialization, roamer support income via meaningful assists/protection/objectives; anti-farming and no stealing carry economy.
34. **Comeback.** Shutdown, capped catch-up EXP, defensive openings and contested objectives; no artificial forced outcome or secret damage boost.
35. **Macro/micro.** AI and player training for aiming, positioning, execution, lane rotation, wave control, resource sharing, split push and coordinated objective prioritization.
36. **Counter matrix.** 78 unordered / 156 directional distinct hero matchups; no absolute counter. Quin example: Quin stone enables optional tradeoff counter configuration, equally available competitive alternatives, no pay advantage. Combinations and duplicate conversion balanced by explicit recipes.
37. **Stone integrity.** Atomic reservation, trade, combine, settlement, refund and idempotency. Cancelled/failed loading, banned/taken picks, interrupted/reconnected match, duplicate requests, concurrent trade and cancelled combination recover correctly. Valid loss consumes one; training free; offline save never authorizes online.
38. **Lifecycle.** Lobby -> queue -> ready -> draft -> prepare -> loading -> active -> terminal -> settlement -> results. Legal transitions, server cancel, reconnect, one terminal result/settlement; stale events rejected.
39. **Networking.** Server ticks, input sequence, prediction/reconciliation, interpolation, bounded lag compensation, snapshot compression, interest filtering, heartbeat, reconnect, takeover and replay. Ping bands illustrative, measure actual loss/jitter and latency; no freeze on one player's disconnect.
40. **Audio.** Original attacks/impact, crit, shield break, heal, skills ready, low HP, eligible ambush cue, directional tower and objective announcements, multi-kill, victory/defeat; First Triumph, Covenant Unbroken, Guardian Fallen, Sentinel Secured, Citadel Under Siege, Final Stand, Covenant Victory are proposed lines.
41. **Animation.** Idle/walk/run/turn, weapon-specific attacks, each skill/ultimate, hit/stun/knockback, death/respawn/recall, victory/defeat, transformations, cloth/hair/trails. Accurate event timing independent of visual FPS. Preserve approved art and motion, no placeholder as final.
42. **AI architecture.** Team commander -> hero utility scoring -> navigation/avoidance -> combat execution. Local behavior trees/utility scoring, no paid inference per decision. Observe same vision, costs and cooldowns.
43. **Communication.** Attack, retreat, gather, missing, defend, ult ready/used, objective timers, help, quick chat, optional team voice, mute and ping rate limits; independent of app dashboard.
44. **Arena Honor.** Completion/recovery, positive teamwork, evidence-based intentional AFK/feed, harassment, cheating, collusion and false reports. No punishment for inexperience or provider outage; appeal/review path.
45. **Anti-cheat.** Server movement/teleport, damage, cooldown, gold, visibility, inventory, match settlement, reward validation; replay/duplicate rejection, risk-based farm/collusion, permission/audit controls, no trusted client reward amounts.
46. **Balance lab.** All matchups, early/mid/late power, mobility, survivability, difficulty, pick/ban/win by skill bracket, counterplay, symmetric faction results. Fixed seeds, side swaps, replayable simulations; no single overall win rate as proof.
47. **Diversity.** 13 choose 2 = 78; choose 3 = 286; choose 5 = 1287; disjoint opposing team lineups = 72,072 ordered or 36,036 unordered before bans; equipment, paths, tactics add diversity. Preserve all 13 existing characters.
48. **Priorities.** P0 offline complete combat, heroes, map, hit detection, effects, waves, towers, jungle, AI, controls, visual fidelity, stones integrity; online P0 authority/reconnect/security. Equipment/talents/draft and ranking mandatory before relevant competitive mode. P1 analytics/replay/audio polish; P2 community/tournaments/special maps. No paid advantages.
49. **Unique expansions.** Covenant Rescue, Last Stand tactical defense (not forced buff), Living Relics visible equipment, Signature Moments, Covenant Mastery, Battle Chronicle and community banners. Cosmetic affiliation, no ranked power.
50. **Tests.** Ten-hero fights, concurrent ultimates, unit load, death/cast order, single objective winner, interruptions/reconnect, duplicate reward/trade, cancel/refund, background/resume, frame-rate equivalence, full AI matches, repeated matches/no leaks, character persistence, existing BUKLOD regression; actual Unity EditMode/PlayMode and Android device acceptance.

### Follow-on combat/economy integration
12 functional match gear items / six unique slots, 60% resale, shop disabled when own core falls, bounded critical/basic lifesteal/attack speed, armor penetration, anti-heal, burst mitigation, HP and mana/CDR equipment, basic HP-based damage. Shutdown and repeated-death gold, scaling respawn delay, damage taken/dealt. Living supplier/crafter now requires gold or materials and only pays on success; revival requires crystal, team pickup gold has a real consumer. Bots select basic defensive/offensive item orders and cease tower avoidance after reinforcement cutoff/core loss. These are initial balancing parameters, not final 48-item recipe/active-equipment system. Source only; UI binding, Unity test execution and online services remain open.

### Offline tactics, status and draft integration
Training now selects ten globally distinct heroes, deterministic by match seed. Pure draft validation requires ten unique players/heroes, five per faction, at most two unique bans and no banned pick. Stone combinations quote two same or three different inputs into one different selected hero; counter quotes require a real opposing hero and owned matching stone. These are preflight only, no client mint/debit. Eleven implemented free tactics use independent cooldowns; Wilderness Mastery awaits neutral objectives. Recall channels for 4 seconds, cancelled by damage, movement or valid action and prohibited after core loss/cutoff. Stun/root/silence/disarm/fear/suppression restrictions, cleanse, CC immunity, untargetable/invulnerable distinction and explicit target-type attack API are implemented. No graphics/HUD binding yet; Unity execution pending.

### Neutral objectives and vision integration
Seven ordinary wilderness creature types mirrored across Lux/Tenebra (14 camps), Ancient Sentinel and Colossal Guardian (16 total). Camps have spawn/respawn, health, bounded retaliation, 7s disengage reset, single kill rewards, seeded drop rarity, resource/vision buffs, and no contribution to faction elimination. Wilderness Mastery is the twelfth tactic; basic attacks may target nearby neutral camps, jungle bots farm while the enemy core survives, bosses grant team gold/EXP and shield or enhanced siege waves. After reinforcement cutoff camps deactivate. Vision tracks allied range, solid cover line-of-sight, four logical concealment zones and attack reveal; core-loss survivors are strategically revealed and overtime exposes both factions to avoid hiding forever, but projectiles/targeting still respect solid cover. Actual fog rendering, neutral models, HUD binding, balancing and Unity execution remain separate gates.
