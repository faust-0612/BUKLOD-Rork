# Arena commerce DEV preview
This isolated branch adds an offline catalogue, 13 Sacred Stone identities, 39 existing palette identities (original/radiant/nightwatch) and 13 legendary and 13 mythical concepts, category browsing and generated category-specific emblems with hero color identity. Review-only meshes are not approved by this change. PHP prices are indicative, not billing SKUs. No purchase, ad request, entitlement, currency mutation, online queue or production access is performed.

Release gates: reconcile integrated APK and hybrid logic branches; approved rigged skin assets and faction visuals; server verified Play receipts and idempotent entitlement/revocation ledger; BP-only marketplace fees; no QMP or cash conversion; no paid strength or paid ranked advantage; no rewarded tradable item; ads only at safe transitions after consent and cooldown; sponsor placements outside combat readability; deterministic season and membership expiry; replay and private event services; Play policy review; Unity compile and device visual checks. Never present proposed price as actual storefront price.

Higher tiers: mythical 999, transformation 1499, exclusive faction 1999, all-hero legendary 4999 PHP. Institutional packages and sponsorship are inquiry concepts, never consumer checkout. Marketplace entry is BP-only. The 190-entry catalogue and its emblems are visual concepts, not licensed or finished 3D skins. A service-only entitlement candidate is supplied separately; it starts disabled and cannot verify Google Play by itself.
