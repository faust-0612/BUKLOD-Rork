# Premium asset acceptance contract
Do not create an approval receipt until actual rights and art review evidence exist. The visual catalog references a TextAsset JSON with rightsVerified (boolean), originalArtReviewed (boolean), and evidence (nonempty references to the rights/visual review records). These declarations do not themselves prove rights or likeness; keep supporting evidence. The catalog requires all 13 distinct skinned hero meshes, at least nine nonempty distinct AnimationClip references per controller, a Speed float and Attack/Primary/Secondary/Ultimate triggers; both faction structures, five minion roles and six villagers per faction; and the Genesis Rift terrain prefab. Passing structural validation is not a visual quality pass. The 39 hero variants, full animation state coverage, VFX/SFX and device performance are separate acceptance requirements. Keep unlicensed review assets outside the runtime catalog.

Hybrid production extension: each faction must have six distinct core/tower/depot/guard post/farm/barracks prefabs. Catalog requires distinct forest, quarry, food, crystal and gold resource prefabs, nine wilderness creature prefabs, and separate field/relic pickup models. Review mode can omit these new assets, but production validation fails closed. Catalog references must not point to ReviewOnly. Actual art/rights review, full-match rendering and device verification remain mandatory.
