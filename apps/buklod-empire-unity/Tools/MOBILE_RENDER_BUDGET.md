# Mobile render budget (DEV)
Only presentation changes; all match units continue simulation, including offscreen bots and villagers. A conservative expanded camera envelope and 32/38 simulation-unit warm/retain radii control creation and rental of visuals; the player and any intersecting unit are always retained. Nearby skill audio range (26) remains inside the warm radius. The 18/22 shadow hysteresis retains original casting modes and restores them before pooling. Animator culls transform updates when invisible; skinned renderers do not update offscreen. Inactive visual cache is bounded globally at 64 and per prefab at 8. Ground elevation sampling is cached until movement exceeds 0.15 units; position interpolation remains per frame. Camera is positioned before first sync. No geometry simplification or mesh LOD has been claimed. The separate NUnit tests encode budget invariants, but require a Unity Editor to run; no device FPS or memory improvement is measured yet. Review with crowded matches, camera transitions, respawn, nearby audio, and terrain elevation before release. Production gate is unchanged.
