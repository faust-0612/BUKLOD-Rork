# Mobile render budget (DEV)
Only presentation changes; all match units continue simulation, including offscreen bots and villagers. A conservative expanded camera envelope and 32/38 simulation-unit warm/retain radii control creation and rental of visuals; the player and any intersecting unit are always retained. Nearby skill audio range (26) remains inside the warm radius. The 18/22 shadow hysteresis retains original casting modes and restores them before pooling. Animator culls transform updates when invisible; skinned renderers do not update offscreen. Inactive visual cache is bounded globally at 64 and per prefab at 8. Ground elevation sampling is cached until movement exceeds 0.15 units; position interpolation remains per frame. Camera is positioned before first sync. No geometry simplification or mesh LOD has been claimed. The separate NUnit tests encode budget invariants, but require a Unity Editor to run; no device FPS or memory improvement is measured yet. Review with crowded matches, camera transitions, respawn, nearby audio, and terrain elevation before release. Production gate is unchanged.

## Actual mobile mesh LOD revision
The lower-tessellation generator produces 13 distinct LOD1 GLBs from the current facial/gear-refined source, reducing total hero triangles from 96,360 to 62,332 (35.3%; each hero at least 30.2%). All original rig, material and animation data comparisons passed offline. `prepare-review-assets.py` automatically generates these files. The candidate importer validates the manifest hashes and attempts to attach each lower mesh to the same animated skeleton and materials with an LODGroup; it rejects inconsistent bind matrices and bones. The 0.055 screen-relative switch threshold is provisional; the final threshold is zero. Only base hero review prefabs are wired to this LOD tier; cosmetic gallery, production assets and faction meshes are not changed. Actual Unity import, switching, deformation and FPS remain unverified. The Android production gate is unchanged.

## Fidelity-first owner direction
Full-detail hero geometry is now the default in BattleView. Existing LOD meshes remain optional fallback; visible heroes retain their authored shadows in premium mode. Offscreen visual pooling and animation culling remain, since they do not reduce onscreen quality. No screen resolution or memory target overrides visual fidelity. The review material generator uses original 256x256 color, roughness and tangent-space normal maps (previously 64x64, no normal maps), with denser major head surfaces. Review camera defaults to high SMAA and a deterministic generated HDR sky reflection cubemap for metallic materials. No external asset purchase, production approval, Unity compilation, device FPS or art acceptance claimed. Final visual and device safety testing remains required.
