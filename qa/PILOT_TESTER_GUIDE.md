# BUKLOD Pilot Tester Guide

Thank you for testing BUKLOD before a wider rollout.

## Your goal

Use the app normally enough to discover:
- what is easy
- what is confusing
- what feels too intrusive
- what is slow or broken
- what could cause accidental oversharing
- what leaders/members may misunderstand

A pilot is successful when it finds problems early—not when testers report that
everything is perfect.

## First login

1. Confirm you are using `BUKLOD Staging`.
2. Do not use your only important production password.
3. Review the privacy/community notices.
4. Verify the correct local church and BUKLOD group.
5. Open Home → Pilot Guide → Pilot Checklist.

## First-week tasks

Try:
- one devotional
- reflection privacy
- Prayer screen/privacy
- BUKLOD Community
- one Learning track
- Church Life
- Directory Privacy
- notifications
- offline/reconnect
- Send Feedback

Leaders should also test the Leader Dashboard and follow-up workflow.

## What NOT to put in technical staging

Do not use:
- real highly confidential pastoral notes
- passwords or verification codes
- unnecessary medical/intimate details
- private information about another person who did not agree to share it

## Reporting a bug

Use Me → Send Feedback.

Include:
- screen/feature
- what you tried
- what happened
- what you expected
- whether you were online/offline

Do not paste sensitive content just to demonstrate the bug.

## Immediate P0 report

Tell the pilot lead immediately if you see:
- someone else's Private reflection
- a cross-church data leak
- unauthorized pastoral note access
- ability to make yourself an admin
- incorrect account deletion
- data corruption
- repeated crash preventing core use
