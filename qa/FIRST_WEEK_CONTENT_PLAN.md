# BUKLOD Pilot — First Week Content Plan

The staging seed schedules a seven-day devotional series beginning on the date the seed
is first applied.

Day 1 — Hindi Ka Nag-iisa sa Paglago
Hebrews 10:24–25
Focus: Christian growth in community.

Day 2 — Maging Tapat sa Maliit
Luke 16:10
Focus: consistency and small faithful habits.

Day 3 — Manalangin para sa Isa't Isa
James 5:16
Focus: prayer and privacy.

Day 4 — Makinig Bago Sumagot
James 1:19
Focus: listening before advice.

Day 5 — Maglingkod nang May Pag-ibig
Galatians 5:13
Focus: ministry as service, not status.

Day 6 — Kapag May Kapatid na Nanghihina
Galatians 6:2
Focus: caring follow-up instead of policing.

Day 7 — Sama-sama sa Misyon
Matthew 28:19–20
Focus: technology serving discipleship and mission.

## Suggested pilot communication cadence

Day 1:
Welcome announcement + Pilot Guide.

Day 2:
Short reminder to review Directory Privacy.

Day 3:
Prayer privacy reminder.

Day 4:
Ask testers to send their first feedback item.

Day 5:
Leader check-in: what saves time / what creates work?

Day 6:
Privacy/RLS retest across multiple accounts.

Day 7:
Pilot feedback request + first-week retrospective.
